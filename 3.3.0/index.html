Chronicle · 3.3.0

3.3.0

The release after 3.1.1. The datapack that shipped with 3.1.1 is still packed in the distribution.

14 November 2026

The full list is the changelog. The changes a running server meets are these.

Rewards follow the rates

Vitality is off by default, so a kill pays the configured experience and SP without a vitality bonus. Set EnableVitality = True in vitality.properties to bring the bonus back. An ordinary monster's rewards follow the contribution of each solo player or party: experience and SP from the HP actually dealt, coins and items from a level-weighted share. A party that is the only side is paid when the monster dies, to members who are alive and in range.

The image

The signed image is published for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. Its base is Debian 13 slim. The distribution adds the native network transports for linux/arm64.

The Java engine

The Java modules moved under engine/java. Their artifact names are unchanged, and the datapack remains a module of the reactor.
